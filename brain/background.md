# Project background

## Why

Homies exists to make recreation a non-negotiable part of life. In India, recreation is too often treated as timepass or an optional indulgence, despite its role in recovery, connection, confidence, play, and everyday wellbeing. The project removes the friction that makes people postpone real-world experiences: discovery, coordination, certainty, booking, and return motivation.

## Goals

- Help people move from "we should do something" to a confirmed real-world plan with as little hassle as possible.
- Make trusted recreation - beginning with arenas and bookable experiences - simple to discover, understand, book, and revisit.
- Encourage repeat real-world participation through fair, transparent recognition such as Tiergies and Vibe status.
- Become the default starting point for recreation in India: play, explore, meet, compete, and unwind.

## Non-goals

- Maximize in-app screen time or create a feed that substitutes browsing for doing.
- Position recreation as a discount-only transaction or a guilt-inducing obligation.
- Fabricate availability, rewards, pricing, or partner inventory; operational facts and loyalty value must remain authoritative and trusted.
- Replace local venues, friendships, or communities rather than making them easier to access.

## Target user

People in India who want a fuller, more active social life but do not want arranging recreation to become another task. This includes busy regulars, group organizers, curious beginners, repeat players, and the venues that need a clearer path from interest to attendance.
